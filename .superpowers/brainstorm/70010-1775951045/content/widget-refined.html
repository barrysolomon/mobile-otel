Interaction Model

Badge

  • 32px circle, semi-transparent dark background
  • Border color = status (green/red/orange)
  • Tap → expand card
  • Long-press + drag → reposition
  • Auto-refresh every 2s

Expanded Card

  • Dark translucent card with 2-col grid
  • Top section: buffer + export (SDK state)
  • Bottom section: device health
  • Session ID + refresh indicator in footer
  • Tap outside or tap badge → collapse
